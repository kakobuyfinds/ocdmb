The LoveGoBuy buying guide

Keep a row for each item. Save the selected variant, seller listing and date checked so you can revisit the same item when warehouse photos arrive.

Check LoveGoBuy product links

For a Weidian product link, Taobao listing or 1688 seller page, match the product photos, model code and color name to your shortlist. Confirm variant availability and the seller’s return window before placing an order.

Compare shoe and clothing measurements

Use a shoe size chart in centimeters and compare the insole length with a pair that fits. For clothing, measure a similar garment flat. Compare hoodie chest width, T-shirt body length, jacket sleeve length, trouser waist and inseam. Check jersey shoulder width and cap circumference where relevant.

Read LoveGoBuy QC photos

Use the warehouse quality check to match the size tag and colorway. Look at outsole wear, left-to-right stitching, zipper alignment and jewelry clasp details. Ask for an additional photo with a ruler when bag dimensions or accessory scale matter.

Compare LoveGoBuy shipping costs

Record domestic delivery, parcel weight and package dimensions. Compare actual weight with volumetric weight where the route uses it. Check available tracking, estimated delivery time and destination-specific charges before dispatch.

Style references

When comparing accessories, look for side and interior views, clear hardware close-ups and a measurement that shows scale.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that organizes product links and comparison details by category. This independent guide sends its category links to CAFANS.

What should I check in warehouse QC photos?

Check the selected color, size label, visible stitching, hardware and any requested measurements against the order. Ask for a clearer photo when a detail is hard to see.

How do I compare the total cost before shipping?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees and the destination-specific shipping quote. Check whether any import charges or currency conversion fees apply.

What should I do if a product link opens a different item?

Compare the seller, product photos and variant with your saved details. Pause the order if the listing has changed and confirm the exact item before paying.
